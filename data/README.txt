Practice data for the Trichuris muris landmark morphometrics tutorial
=====================================================================

All values are SIMULATED and illustrative. They are not real measurements.
Everyone uses the same files, so every student gets identical numbers and figures.
Generated by make_simulated_data.py (fixed random seed 2026).

FILES
-----
trichuris_muris_landmarks_messy.csv
  A spreadsheet-style file, untidy on purpose. 238 rows x 22 columns
  (230 worms + 8 completely empty rows at the bottom).
  - Stage and sex combined in one "Lifestage" column ("Adult - Female", "L1 - newly hatched", ...)
  - Long headers with spaces and symbols; "% of Total" columns that can be calculated
  - Two stray trailing spaces in lifestage labels
  - Two transcription errors in the "Anterior body end to Entrance of St" column
  Section 3 of the tutorial looks at its problems (it is not converted).

trichuris_muris_landmarks_tidy.csv
  The same 230 worms in the tidy layout of the template workbook. 230 rows x 16 columns.
  Columns: sample, host_id, dpi, lifestage, sex, source, wb_length_um, ant_nr_um, ant_st_um,
           nr_st_um, st_um, intestine_um, ov_ts_um, rt_cl_um, rest_um, st_number
  Lengths are in micrometres (um). A blank cell means "not measured" or "not applicable".
  Sections 3.4 onward start from this file.

DESIGN (230 worms, 28 mice)
---------------------------
dpi  lifestage             worms  mice
 0   L1 (newly hatched)      30    none (hatched in vitro: host_id is blank)
 3   L1                      20    4
 9   L2                      20    4
14   L3                      25    5
21   L4                      25    5
35   Adult female / male     30 + 30   5
42   Adult female / male     25 + 25   5

Each mouse contributes worms at a single timepoint. Mice differ in worm size and relative
stichosome length, so worms from the same mouse resemble each other. Each worm was measured
for only a subset of landmarks (length only, head landmarks only, or everything).
