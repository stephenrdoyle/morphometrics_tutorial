Trichuris muris landmark morphometrics tutorial (RStudio project)
==================================================================

0. New to R? Install R and RStudio first (instructions under "Getting started" at the top of the tutorial),
   then work through Appendix A, "R in ten minutes" (at the end of the tutorial), before Section 0. Allow about 6 to 8 hours over three sessions.
1. Double-click  trichuris_morphometrics_tutorial.Rproj  to open the project in RStudio.
2. Open  trichuris_landmark_morphometrics_tutorial.Rmd  and follow the tutorial.
   - Run chunks one at a time (green play button, or Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Enter), or
   - click Knit to build the whole tutorial as an HTML page.
3. The first time, install the packages listed in Section 1 of the tutorial.

Folders
  data/       the two practice CSV files (simulated, not real measurements) and a README
  template/   an Excel template for entering your own worm measurements in the tidy layout
  tools/      the Python script that generated the practice data (not needed to follow the tutorial)
  expected_figures/  what each figure should look like once you have run its chunk.
                     The knitted HTML shows the same images in collapsed "Check your figure" boxes.

Everyone who uses these files gets identical numbers and figures.
The practice data are simulated and illustrative: they are NOT real measurements.
